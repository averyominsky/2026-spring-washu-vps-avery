Something I appreciate from the past is the time I spent with my brother and family during high school. When I was applying to colleges, I did not want to go to the same school as my twin brother.Before graduating, I was obsessed with gaining independence and moving away from home. When my brother got accepted to WashU, I remember feeling upset and not wanting him to go with me. I didn’t fully value those everyday moments with my family. Now, I realize how meaningful that time was, especially because we’ll never live together again in the same way. My brother and I used to drive to school together every day, and even on mornings when I was exhausted, those car rides became something special. Now that we go to different schools, this chapter of life has ended. I cherish my childhood's simplicity, which was easy to overlook at the time.
