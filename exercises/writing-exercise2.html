A human-made creation that brings out the best in me is music. I especially love music when I’m listening through my AirPods on mute mode because I feel fully in my own world. I am drawn to house and calm beats, but I am able to connect with all genres. Music brings out a sense of happiness and emotional connection in me. I enjoy connecting with my friends over music as well as obsessing over specific music artists. Sharing songs, talking about new releases, or getting excited about an upcoming album drop creates a sense of community. Also, music is something that strongly ties to my memory. I often associate songs with a specific moment, feeling, or person. This makes music feel personal and grounding. In addition, music can make me feel like I am in a flow state. I listen to music as much as possible, doing homework, working on art, and walking to class. The rhythms I like listening to keep me focused and relaxed. Not only is the sound of music uplifting, but so is the creativity behind it. Music is inspiring, which is really important to me as a creative person.
