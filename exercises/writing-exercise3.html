I cherish the slow or hard way of doing something when the process matters more than just getting a quick result. Cooking is a good example for me. The best food is usually not rushed, and taking time to be thorough with each step makes a difference. I want food to be good right away, and when it is not it can feel frustrating. When I slow down I can learn what I’m doing instead of just following something to get it done. A mindset of wanting to master things right away has definitely prevented me from trying new things. However, taking the slower approach is how you build the basics that let you improve.
