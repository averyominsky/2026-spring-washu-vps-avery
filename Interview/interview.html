Interdisciplinary Connections Interview

Interviewee

Jacob Ominsky – Math and CS student at Duke University

Interviewer

Avery Ominsky – Communication Design student at WashU

Context

My twin brother and I grew up sharing experiences and perspectives from different disciplines. This conversation explores how math and computer science inform creative thinking, and how art can inspire analytical fields.

1. Interdisciplinary Connections

Goal: How can math/CS and art/design inspire each other.

  • How do you think art and design can benefit from math or computer science?
  • Have you ever used math or code to create art, visuals, or music?
  • Can you think of patterns or principles in math that feel like design principles?

2. Daily Life

Goal: How does math/CS shape approach to life.

  • How does thinking like a mathematician or programmer shape your daily life?
  • Are there ways that logic or problem-solving influence how you make decisions outside of school or work?
  • What habits or mindset help you stay sharp in abstract fields?

3. Creativity, Problem-Solving, and Innovation

Goal: Highlight similarities between creative and analytical thinking.

  • When solving a complex problem, do you ever approach it like a designer would?
  • How do intuition and structured thinking work together in your projects?
  • Are there moments when you feel math or CS is “artistic”?

4. Looking Forward

Goal: Explore the future and personal excitement.

  • What excites you most about the future of math or computer science?
  • Are there technologies, ideas, or problems that you hope artists and designers might collaborate on?
  • How do you see math and art intersecting in the next decade?

Reflection

My twin brother's world of math and programming overlaps with creativity and art.
