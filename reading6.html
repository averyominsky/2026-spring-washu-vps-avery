Reading The Coming Age of Calm Technology made me reflect on my personal relationship with technology. The description of the personal computer as something “intimate” resonated with me. My computer feels like an extension of myself because it holds my studio work, communication, and my personal memories. It is a tool I use constantly and everyday. In that sense, I agree with the idea that the relationship we have with technology matters more than the technology itself. Additionally, the article’s discussion of the Internet as a force of interconnection stands out. Technology links people and information, which shape society. The line about technology bombarding us “frenetically” stands out because it captures the reality of messages and social media. I have sometimes felt that technology has had control over me as it can be addicting. On the other hand, the author compares calm technology to a good pair of shoes which is optimistic. Not all technology creates stress. “Calm technology” is an intriguing discussion, especially the point that good designed information can actually feel calming. Overall, this reading made me realize that the problem isn’t technology itself, but how it’s designed.
