Sacasas’s “Do Artifacts Have Ethics?” emphasizes that technology carries a moral dimension. I don’t fully agree with the framing of the argument that tech workers don’t understand the emotional aspect of their work. The opening quote “we don’t understand the moral imperative of what we do,” feels like a strong generalization. I think people in tech may not prioritize ethics because the system rewards other things more, like speed and profit. I agree with the idea that there should be an emotional and moral dimension to products. Sacasas argues that the moral dimension is already built in, whether we notice it or not. I appreciate the point that we often focus only on how technology is used, like the “guns don’t kill people” argument. Overall, I don’t think the problem is that people in tech lack moral awareness. People need to be encouraged to think ethically. The questions provided will be a helpful reference for the Standby Mode project.
